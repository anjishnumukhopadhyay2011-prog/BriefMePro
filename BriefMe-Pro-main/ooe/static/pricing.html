Common questions

Is there a free tier?
Yes. The live demo is read-only access to the globe and the last 24 hours of events. No signup. Pro unlocks personalisation, alerts, AI scenarios, and the full 90-day history.
Can I cancel?
Anytime, from Account → Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.
Do you offer student / academic discounts?
Yes — email billing@briefme.pro from your .edu address with a screenshot of your student ID for 50% off.
What payment methods?
Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) globally. Razorpay (UPI, NetBanking, cards) in India.
